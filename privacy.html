Privacy

This site is run by Gabe Giro (contact: info@gabegiro.com).

What is collected. This site has no forms and no accounts. If you arrived from an ad, the ad's campaign name (for example "linkedin / fc-2026-10") is kept in your browser and passed to Calendly, so a booked call shows which ad it came from. Google Analytics counts visits without cookies until you choose. If you click "Accept", Google Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and LinkedIn conversion tags may set cookies to measure whether a visit from their ad led to a booked call. They are not used for remarketing. If you decline, no advertising cookies are set. Booking happens on Calendly, which has its own privacy policy.

What is not collected. No personal data is stored on this site, and nothing is sold or shared beyond the services named above.

Your rights. Email the address above to ask what data is held about you, or to have it deleted.
