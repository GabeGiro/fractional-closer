How it works

STEP 1

Offer handover

Your offer, price list, ICP, top objections and best past closes.

STEP 2

2-week prep

I go through your call recordings, sales material and case studies, then build the call script and objection-handling playbook. Not billed.

STEP 3

I close

Discovery and closing calls, 1-call or 2-call close, follow-up sequences until a yes or a no - in your name, in your CRM.

STEP 4

EOD + weekly numbers

Every call logged the same day. Weekly: show rate, close rate, cash collected, deals in follow-up.

Questions

Do you set the calls too?

No - I close the calls your team or funnel books. No lead flow yet? I can connect you with a lead-gen partner.

When is commission paid?

Every 2 weeks, and only on cash collected.

What's a prequalified call?

One your team or funnel already qualified for budget, need and timing: 10% of cash collected. If I do the qualifying, 15%.

What if you don't close?

Month 1 is ramp-up. If my close rate is under 25% in month 2, you get 100% of your plan fees back.

Do you close under my brand?

Yes - your email domain, your CRM, your price list. Your buyers just meet your closer.

How fast can you be on my calls?

2 weeks of prep after the handover, then I'm on your calendar. The first billed month starts with the first calls.

We already have closers - why you?

When your closers' calendars are full, qualified calls go cold. I take the overflow under your brand - no new headcount, no 3-6 months of ramp.

Can I stop?

Yes, with 30 days' notice after the 2-month minimum.
